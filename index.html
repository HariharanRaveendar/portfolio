About

From microflows to backend services

I'm an MCA graduate from the College of Engineering, Guindy (Anna University), and a 3x Certified Mendix Advanced Developer with over five years of experience building and customizing enterprise applications on the Mendix low-code platform.

My work spans application modelling, microflow design, custom widgets, and integrating external systems — SSO, LDAP, SAP, OData, field and file encryption, and payment gateways among them — for clients including government departments in the UAE, a bank in Bahrain, and a refinery in India.

Lately I've been moving into full-stack development with Node.js, NestJS, Prisma, and PostgreSQL — debugging production infrastructure, working with object storage (MinIO) and search (OpenSearch), and building backend services that scale. I thrive in collaborative environments and enjoy partnering with stakeholders to turn a request into a working system.
